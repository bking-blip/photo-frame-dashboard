# Classic Sage and Sausage Stuffing (Dressing)

Dried bread cubes, sage sausage, onion, celery, and herbs bound with stock and eggs, baked covered then crisped.

- Author: J. Kenji López-Alt
- Site: Serious Eats
- Source: https://www.seriouseats.com/recipes/2010/11/classic-sage-and-sausage-stuffing-or-dressing-recipe.html
- Category: Side / Holiday
- Serves: 10 to 14
- Time: 10 min prep, 2 hr 15 min cook, about 2 hr 50 min total
- Oven: 275°F to dry bread, then 350°F
- Equipment: 2 rimmed baking sheets, Dutch oven, 9x13 dish (or 10x14 oval), instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 lb (1.25 kg) good sandwich bread or soft Italian/French bread (about 2 loaves), cut in 3/4-inch dice (about 5 quarts)
- 8 Tbsp (1 stick, 115 g) butter
- 1 1/2 lb (680 g) sage sausage, out of casing
- 1 large onion, finely chopped (about 12 oz)
- 4 large ribs celery, finely chopped (about 12 oz)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1/4 cup minced fresh sage (or 2 tsp dried)
- Kosher salt and black pepper, if needed
- 1 quart (4 cups) low-sodium chicken or turkey stock, divided
- 3 large eggs
- 1/4 cup minced parsley, divided

## Steps

1. **Dry the bread.** Racks at lower-middle and upper-middle, oven to 275°F. Spread bread over 2 rimmed sheets and bake about 50 min until completely dry, rotating trays and stirring several times. Cool. Raise oven to 350°F.
2. **Sausage.** In a large Dutch oven, melt butter over medium-high until foaming subsides (don't brown it), about 2 min. Add sausage and mash with a stiff whisk or potato masher into fine pieces, no bigger than 1/4 inch. Cook, stirring often, until only a few bits of pink remain, about 8 min.
3. **Vegetables.** Add onion, celery, garlic, and sage. Cook, stirring often, until softened, about 10 min. Off heat, add half the stock.
4. **Bind.** Whisk remaining stock, eggs, and 3 Tbsp parsley until smooth. Stirring constantly, slowly pour into the sausage mixture. Add bread and fold gently until evenly mixed.
5. **Bake covered.** Stuff the turkey with some if you want. Put the rest in a buttered 9x13, cover tightly with foil, and bake until the center reads 150°F, about 45 min.
6. **Crisp.** Remove foil and bake 15 to 20 min more until golden and crisp on top.
7. **Serve.** Cool 5 min, sprinkle with remaining parsley.

## Notes

- Dried or fresh fruit and nuts can be folded in with the bread cubes in step 4.
- Makes enough to stuff two to three 18 to 22 lb birds. Bake any extra in a buttered dish.
- With homemade or low-sodium stock, season to taste with salt and pepper before adding.
- Can be assembled through step 4 and held in the greased dish the day before.
