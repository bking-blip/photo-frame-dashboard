# Easy Cranberry Sauce

Five-ingredient cranberry sauce with orange, brown sugar, and a little vanilla. Thickens as it cools.

- Author: Sally McKenney
- Site: Sally's Baking Addiction
- Source: https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/cranberry-sauce/
- Category: Side / Holiday
- Yield: 2 cups
- Time: 5 min prep, 20 min cook

## Ingredients

- 12 oz bag cranberries, fresh or frozen, rinsed
- 3/4 cup (150 g) water
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) fresh orange juice (about 1/2 large orange)
- 3/4 cup (150 g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract

## Steps

1. **Orange first.** Zest the orange before cutting it, then juice it. Half a large orange gives about 1/4 cup.
2. **Reserve berries.** After rinsing, set 1/2 cup cranberries aside for the end.
3. **Simmer.** Combine the rest of the cranberries, water, orange juice, and brown sugar in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Stir occasionally until simmering.
4. **Reduce.** Drop to low-medium and cook, stirring occasionally, about 15 min until the berries burst and the liquid thickens.
5. **Finish.** Off heat, stir in the reserved cranberries, zest, and vanilla. It keeps thickening as it cools.
6. **Serve.** Warm or room temp. Refrigerate leftovers up to 5 days.

## Notes

- Frozen cranberries go in straight from the freezer. No need to dry them after rinsing.
- A 12 oz bag is 3 to 4 cups of berries.
- 3/4 cup sugar is moderately sweet. Go up to 1 cup (200 g) for a sweeter sauce.
- Make ahead up to 3 days, covered in the fridge. Freezes up to 3 months; thaw in the fridge.
