# Recipe Title

One or two plain sentences describing the dish.

- Author: Person who wrote the recipe
- Site: Website or cookbook name
- Source: https://full-link-to-the-original-recipe
- Category: Side / Holiday
- Serves: 4 to 6
- Time: 20 min prep, 90 min bake
- Oven: 400°F
- Equipment: anything unusual the recipe needs
