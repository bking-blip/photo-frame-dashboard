# Hasselback Potato Gratin

Potato slices stood on edge in a cream and cheese bath, so you get crisp tops and creamy centers in every bite.

- Author: J. Kenji López-Alt
- Site: Serious Eats
- Source: https://www.seriouseats.com/recipes/2013/12/hasselback-potato-gratin-casserole-holiday-food-lab.html
- Category: Side / Holiday
- Serves: 4 to 6
- Time: 20 min prep, 90 min bake, about 110 min total
- Oven: 400°F
- Equipment: 2-quart baking dish, mandoline

## Ingredients

- 3 oz (85 g) Gruyère or Comté, finely grated
- 2 oz (60 g) Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
- 2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream
- 1 Tbsp fresh thyme leaves, roughly chopped
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 to 3 1/2 lb (1.4 to 1.6 kg) russet potatoes, peeled and sliced 1/8 inch on a mandoline (5 to 6 medium)
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter

## Steps

1. **Prep.** Rack in middle, oven to 400°F. Butter a 2-quart baking dish. Combine both cheeses in a large bowl, then move 1/3 of it to a separate bowl and set aside.
2. **Coat.** Add cream, thyme, and garlic to the big bowl of cheese. Season generously with salt and pepper. Add potato slices and toss by hand until every slice is coated, separating any that stick together.
3. **Arrange.** Grab a handful, square it into a neat stack, and set it in the dish with edges vertical. Work around the perimeter and into the center. Pack very tightly. Slice and coat more potato if needed.
4. **Add liquid.** Pour leftover cream mixture over the potatoes until it comes halfway up the sides. You may not need all of it.
5. **Bake covered.** Cover tightly with foil and bake 30 min.
6. **Bake uncovered.** Remove foil and bake about 30 min more, until the top is pale golden.
7. **Cheese top.** Sprinkle the reserved cheese over the top and bake about 30 min more, until deep golden brown and crisp.
8. **Rest.** Let sit a few minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Tighter packing is better. Loose potatoes slump and don't crisp on top.
